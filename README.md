### 🚨 THE PROBLEM
- 70% of emerging human diseases come from animals
- In Northern Nigeria, a herder's sick cow, a child's fever, and a drying river are connected — but no system connects them
- Wrong drug dosages kill livestock and cause antimicrobial resistance
- Farmers lose 40% income to preventable diseases

### 💡 OUR SOLUTION — 4 CORE AI MODULES
**1. Zoonotic Early Warning System** — Predicts outbreaks 14 days before using climate, animal movement, and symptom data.  
**2. Precision Dosage Calculator** — Calculates exact drug dose by weight, species, age. Works OFFLINE for rural areas.  
**3. Climate-Health Intelligence** — Correlates rainfall, temperature, disease patterns to guide pastoralists.  
**4. OneHealth Knowledge Hub** — Voice assistant in Hausa, Fulfulde, and English for low-literacy farmers.
